# Amit 2.0 Content Bank

This is the editorial source of truth for researched topics, audience problems, ideas, hooks, formats, evidence and performance learnings.

## Core audience

- Primary: designers with 0-5 years.
- Secondary: designers with 5-10+ years.
- Audience needs: employability, portfolio quality, craft, product thinking, AI fluency, career direction, communication and mentorship.

## Six recurring franchises

1. Designer, Decode This - industry reality and career perspectives.
2. The Design Gym - practical craft, frameworks and checklists.
3. AI x Design Lab - AI tools, workflows, agentic experiences and experiments.
4. From My Design Diary - experience-based stories and lessons.
5. The Design Clinic - portfolio reviews, critique and career dilemmas.
6. The Creative Shelf - books, references, people and inspiration.

## Editorial mix

20% Decode This, 20% Design Gym, 20% AI x Design Lab, 15% Design Diary, 15% Design Clinic, 10% Creative Shelf. Treat this as a starting hypothesis.

## Idea record template

Each idea should capture:
- Topic / working title
- Audience problem
- Audience segment
- Franchise
- Hook variants (at least 3)
- Format recommendation
- Useful takeaway
- Original point of view
- Evidence / source URLs
- Source date and research date
- Trend signal and trend confidence
- Production effort
- Expected action (watch, save, share, comment, DM)
- Status and publish date
- Performance after 24h and 7d

### Scoring model

Score each candidate from 0-5:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Audience relevance | 25% |
| Timeliness / trend momentum | 20% |
| Utility / save potential | 15% |
| Shareability | 15% |
| Originality / distinct POV | 10% |
| Credibility / evidence | 10% |
| Production feasibility | 5% |

Weighted score = sum(score/5 × weight). Penalise unsupported claims, poor audience fit and stale trend evidence. Display evidence quality and confidence separately; do not hide weak data behind a high score.

## Monthly operating loop

1. Refresh trend signals and source dates.
2. Score existing ideas and new candidate topics.
3. Review the top 10 with human editorial judgement.
4. Select 4 weekly ideas across franchise/format mix.
5. Produce and publish.
6. Capture 24-hour and 7-day performance.
7. Feed results back into the next scoring cycle.
