04 / Design principles

Rules of the road
01One dominant idea per frame. Don't make every point a headline.
02Use contrast and hierarchy before adding decoration.
03Signal is an accent, not a background colour for everything.
04Keep copy short enough to read on a phone without effort.
05Use grids, rules, labels and numbering to create editorial rhythm.
06Motion reveals information. It does not exist just to look busy.
07Every format carries the same identity, adapted to its job.
08Let the idea lead. The visual system should make it clearer.
