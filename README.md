# Social2.0

A content creation and growth workspace for Amit 2.0's Instagram revival.

## Workspaces
- [Amit 2.0 Visual Kit](./Amit-2.0-Visual-Kit/README.md): design tokens, typography, templates and motion language.
- [Amit 2.0 Content Bank](./Amit-2.0-Content-Bank/README.md): researched ideas, editorial system and scoring engine.
- [Amit 2.0 MotionKit](./Amit-2.0-MotionKit/README.md): reusable HTML/CSS/JavaScript Reel generator.
- [Social Viral Content Engine](./Social-Viral-Content-Engine/README.md): choose between backlog ideas and evidence-backed timely opportunities.

## Social Viral Content Engine
The engine compares the existing idea bank with new trend candidates and audience signals using explainable scoring:
- Audience relevance 25%
- Timeliness / trend momentum 20%
- Utility / save potential 15%
- Shareability 15%
- Originality / Amit POV 10%
- Credibility / evidence 10%
- Production feasibility 5%

It presents a shortlist and rationale; Amit makes the final editorial decision. A trend must include source URL, source date, checked date, scope and confidence. If no provider is connected, show "trend feed not connected" and rank the backlog only. Do not fabricate trend momentum or claim virality without evidence.

## Publishing guardrails
- Four primary posts/week: Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Sunday.
- Two Reels + two carousels per week.
- Minimal face-to-camera content.
- Two revision cycles maximum.
- Human review before publication.
- Never buy followers, use engagement pods or scrape restricted/private data.
