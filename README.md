# Social2.0

A content creation and growth workspace for Amit 2.0's Instagram revival.

## Workspaces

- [Amit 2.0 Visual Kit](./Amit-2.0-Visual-Kit/README.md): visual identity, templates, typography, colour and motion principles.
- [Amit 2.0 Content Bank](./Amit-2.0-Content-Bank/README.md): researched ideas, audience problems, hooks, editorial calendar and the Social Viral Content Engine.
- [Amit 2.0 MotionKit](./Amit-2.0-MotionKit/README.md): reusable HTML/CSS/JavaScript Reel generator.

## Social Viral Content Engine

The engine ranks the 40 researched ideas alongside newly discovered trends. It uses:
1. Audience relevance (0-5 years primary, up to 10+ secondary)
2. Timeliness and trend momentum
3. Usefulness / save potential
4. Shareability
5. Originality of Amit's perspective
6. Credibility / evidence quality
7. Production effort and format fit

It produces a ranked shortlist and a recommendation with a rationale. Trend discovery is intentionally a pluggable research input: connect a current source (Instagram/Google Trends/industry news/social listening) and record source URL, observed date and evidence. Do not present guessed topics or stale data as current trends.

## Principles

- 4 primary posts/week: Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Sunday.
- 2 Reels + 2 carousels each week.
- Minimal personal visibility; editorial and kinetic typography-led content.
- Useful perspective over decorative polish.
- Two revision cycles maximum.
- Small, test-led budget; never buy followers or use engagement pods.
